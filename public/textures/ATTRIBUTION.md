# Planet texture credits

- Earth, Moon, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune: NASA 3D Resources. NASA imagery and texture resources.
- Sun, Mercury, Uranus, Earth clouds and Saturn rings: Solar System Scope texture library, CC BY 4.0.
- Solar System Scope: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures
- NASA 3D Resources: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/

Textures have been sized for real-time web rendering. Planet distances and sizes in the experience are intentionally compressed for cinematic visibility and are not to scale.
